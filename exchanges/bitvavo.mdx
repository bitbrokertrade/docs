To let BitBrokerTrade trade on your Bitvavo account, first create an API key in Bitvavo and then add it to BitBrokerTrade. This process has three parts: creating the API key, adding the exchange, and connecting a trader.

<Warning>
  After you create an API key, Bitvavo sends a confirmation email. **Click the confirmation link in your inbox first, before adding the exchange in BitBrokerTrade.** If you skip this step, the connection will fail.
</Warning>

## Part 1: Create an API key in Bitvavo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the API settings">
    Log in to [bitvavo.com](https://bitvavo.com/invite?a=98284B37AF) and click your **account name** in the top right. From the dropdown menu, choose **"API"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new key">
    Click **"Add new API key"**. A form appears for your new key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set permissions">
    Give the key a recognizable name, for example **"API BitBrokerTrade"**. Then enable the toggles for **"Read only"** and **"Trade digital assets"** (slide them to the right). Leave the **IP whitelist** field empty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate the key">
    Click **"Generate API key"**. Enter your Bitvavo password and your 2FA authentication code, and click **Confirm**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the keys">
    Click the copy icon next to the **API key** to copy it to your clipboard. Paste the key in a notepad. Do the same with the **API secret key**. Then click **"I have saved the secret API secret"**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the confirmation email">
    Bitvavo now sends an email to your registered address. **Open the email and click the confirmation link.** Only then does the API key become active in Bitvavo.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Part 2: Add the exchange in BitBrokerTrade

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Exchanges">
    Log in to BitBrokerTrade. In the left column, click **Settings**, then **Exchanges**, and then **Add exchange**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Bitvavo">
    Click the **Bitvavo logo** to choose Bitvavo as the exchange.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the details">
    In the **Name** field, enter `Bitvavo`. Then paste the previously copied **API key** and **API secret** into the matching fields.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a reserved amount (optional)">
    Do you want to keep part of your balance out of the trader? Enable **"Reserved amount"** and enter the desired amount in euros. This amount is never used for purchases.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the exchange">
    Check that you have already confirmed the Bitvavo email. Then click **Add**. The connection is now active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Reserved amount, how does it work?** Say you have €1,000 on Bitvavo and you set €250 as the reserved amount. The trader will then only work with €750. A balance of €250 will always remain untouched on your euro account.
</Note>

## Part 3: Connect a trader

Now that the exchange has been added, the next step is to connect a trader to your Bitvavo account. Follow the full step-by-step guide on the [Trader settings](/traders/trader-settings) page.
