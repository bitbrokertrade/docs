BitBrokerTrade is trader software that automatically trades cryptocurrency for you 24 hours a day, 365 days a year through your own account at an exchange. You don't have to do anything yourself: the software recognizes upward and downward price trends and executes buy and sell orders at the right moment.

## Get started in five steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up with BitBrokerTrade">
    Create a free account at [bitbroker.trade](https://bitbroker.trade/Authentication/Register). You only need an email address to get started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open an account at an exchange">
    Open an account at one of the six supported exchanges: Bitvavo, Binance, Bybit, Bitstamp, Coinbase, or KuCoin. Deposit euros into your account so the trader can trade.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the exchange to BitBrokerTrade">
    Generate an API key on your exchange and add it to BitBrokerTrade via **Settings → Exchanges → Add exchange**. The connection is tested immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure and activate a trader">
    Create a trader via **Settings → Traders → Add trader**. Choose your exchange, coin, strategy, and return target. Activate the trader, and it starts analyzing right away.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track results via the dashboard">
    View your open positions, sell transactions, and total profit in real time via the dashboard. The trader keeps working while you do whatever you want.
  </Step>
</Steps>
